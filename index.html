How PPG works

PPG means "points per game". We add every point a player scores in the selected month, then divide that total by the number of games they played. This is to prevent one bad day from ruining the entire score.

What MGP means

MGP means minimum games played. A player must reach the MGP threshold to qualify for the official rankings, which keeps the table fair for players with enough monthly match volume.

How rankings work

Official rankings include only players who meet MGP, and they are sorted by highest PPG first. Players below the MGP threshold appear in the In Progress section until they qualify for the main board.
