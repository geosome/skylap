How It Works
The League

01 What is Court Club?

Court Club is a friendly local badminton club tracking monthly standings across two courts. Up to 8 regular players compete in doubles matches each session, with standings updated after every game. A fresh leaderboard resets each month — keeping competition alive all year round.

Scoring System

02 Points Per Game (PPG)

Rankings are determined by PPG — Points Per Game average. Every player is credited with their team's score in each match they play. PPG is then calculated as:

PPG = Total Points Scored ÷ Games Played

For example, if you played 5 games and your teams scored 21, 15, 21, 18, and 21 — your total is 96 points, giving you a PPG of 19.20.

03 Match Format

Each match is a doubles game — Team A (2 players) vs Team B (2 players). The winning team's score and the losing team's score are each assigned to every player on the respective team. Guest players may join any session but their scores are not counted toward the official standings.

Rankings Rules

04 Minimum Games Played (MGP)

To appear in the Official Rankings, a player must have participated on at least 15 distinct calendar days in the current month. This ensures only players who show up consistently are ranked — regardless of how many games they play per session.

Players who haven't yet reached 15 days appear in the In Progress section below the official standings, showing their current day count toward qualification.

05 Monthly Reset

Standings are filtered by month. All match history is preserved — use the month selector in the header to view any previous month's standings. Rankings start fresh each month, so every player has an equal shot at the top spot.
